# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors should be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing
viewpoints, and avoid personal attacks, discriminatory language, harassment,
or publication of another person's private information.

## Project data boundary

Do not submit real hotel-review text, account identifiers, credentials,
cookies, or other personal data in issues, pull requests, fixtures, or logs.
Use synthetic, minimal reproductions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe
violations. Report conduct or security concerns privately through the
repository's GitHub Security Advisory form. Maintainers will handle reports as
confidentially as reasonably possible and will avoid conflicts of interest.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
